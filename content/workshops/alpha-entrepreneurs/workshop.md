## Mastery — what every kid walks out able to do

Workshops are a blast — and every kid walks out having actually done something. Below is the bar.

1. Define a real customer and the specific problem you solve for them.
2. Set a price and justify it against costs (not vibes).
3. Pitch the business in 90 seconds without notes.
4. Take one hard question from a "judge" and answer it directly.
