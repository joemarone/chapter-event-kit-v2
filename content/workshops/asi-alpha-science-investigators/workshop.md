## Mastery — what every kid walks out able to do

Workshops are a blast — and every kid walks out having actually done something. Below is the bar.

1. Form a hypothesis based on at least three pieces of evidence.
2. Identify which evidence supports it and which contradicts it.
3. Present the case to the group with a clear chain of reasoning.
4. Concede a point when challenged with stronger evidence.
