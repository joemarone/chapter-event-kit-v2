# Run-of-Show — ASI: Alpha Science Investigators

**Total runtime: 90 minutes**

---

## Before kids arrive (20 min prep)

- [ ] **Stage the crime scene.** Pick one corner of the room. Lay out: a knocked-over chair, a "note" with ambiguous handwriting, a coffee cup with lipstick, a shoe print (flour + shoe template), a dropped key, a torn piece of fabric.
- [ ] **Build 4 suspect dossiers.** One page each — a name, a photo (stock), an alibi, and one detail that either matches or contradicts the evidence. Two suspects should be plausible.
- [ ] **Pre-assemble evidence bags.** One ziplock per evidence item, labeled with a letter (A, B, C, D, E, F).
- [ ] Tape off the crime scene with string or masking tape.

## 0:00 — 0:05 · Brief the investigators

- Kids gather.
- **Say this:** "A thing happened in this room before you got here. I know what happened. You don't. Your job: figure it out from what's left behind, and convince me."
- Hand out badges (printed, kid writes name). No giggling — badges make it real.

## 0:05 — 0:20 · Scene walk and evidence collection

- Teams of 3 (four teams of 3).
- Each team walks the scene **once**, for 3 minutes. Observes only. No touching.
- After each team has walked, they come to the evidence table and get **copies** of all evidence — photos, transcripts of the note, a plaster cast of the shoe print.
- **Mastery Gate #1:** each team must log at least 3 observations per evidence item before moving on. Check their sheets.

## 0:20 — 0:40 · Suspect review

- Hand out the 4 suspect dossiers to each team.
- Teams cross-reference evidence against alibis.
- You circulate. One rule: you only answer **yes/no questions**. "Does the shoe size on Suspect B's dossier match the footprint?" → "Yes." "Who did it?" → "Nice try."

## 0:40 — 1:00 · Build the case

- Each team picks their suspect and writes a 3-point case.
- **Mastery Gate #2:** the case sheet must list (a) the hypothesis, (b) three pieces of supporting evidence, (c) one piece of contradicting evidence they can explain away.
- No case sheet = no presentation.

## 1:00 — 1:20 · Present and cross-examine

- Each team presents for 3 minutes.
- **You are the defense attorney.** After each presentation, you challenge one piece of their reasoning. Push hard.
- **Mastery Gate #3:** the team has to either defend it or concede the point. A good concession counts as mastery — that's the Critical Thinking muscle.

## 1:20 — 1:30 · The reveal and debrief

- You reveal what actually happened.
- Circle up. Two questions:
  1. Did any team get it right? If not — why did the evidence lead somewhere else?
  2. What was one thing you noticed in the scene that you initially dismissed?

## Common failures and fast fixes

- **"We all picked the same suspect in 2 minutes"** → Too easy. Plant a new piece of evidence mid-workshop.
- **"We can't agree"** → Good. Make them vote. Minority writes a dissent paragraph — counts toward Mastery Gate #4.
- **"I don't want to present"** → Every team member says one sentence. Not optional. The shy kid's sentence can be the simplest one.

## Clean-up (5 min, off the clock)

- All evidence back in bags. Scene goes down. Badges stay with the kids.
