# Run-of-Show — Actions Speak Louder

**Total runtime: 90 minutes**

---

## 0:00 — 0:10 · Warm-up circle
- Everyone stands. Pass a soft ball around. When you catch it, say your name + one thing you could talk about for 5 minutes straight.
- No judging anyone's choice. Lasagna, Minecraft, baby siblings — all fair.

## 0:10 — 0:20 · The 60-second frame
- Teach one thing: **Beginning, Middle, End.** Write it on a board.
- Beginning = where and who. Middle = the problem or twist. End = what changed.
- Each kid gets an index card. Write 3 words for each part. That's their whole outline.

## 0:20 — 0:40 · First tellings
- Round the room. Every kid stands, delivers their 60-second story.
- Rule: no "um," "like," or "so" at the start of sentences. If one slips out, the kid just pauses and keeps going. Pauses are fine. Fillers are not.
- **Mastery Gate #1:** every kid has to get through the full 60 seconds.

## 0:40 — 0:55 · Eye contact drill
- Pair kids up. Kid A tells their story again. Kid B holds up 3 fingers and drops one every time A makes real eye contact with someone across the room.
- **Mastery Gate #2:** three sustained eye-contacts per story.

## 0:55 — 1:15 · Interruption practice
- You, the organizer, will deliberately interrupt each kid mid-story once. Drop a book. Ask a random question. Walk past.
- Kid must pause, acknowledge, then return to the story.
- **Mastery Gate #3:** recovery without restarting.

## 1:15 — 1:25 · Q&A round
- Final delivery. Every kid tells their story one more time, for real.
- After each, audience asks **one** question. Kid answers on the spot. No "I don't know" allowed — they have to try.
- **Mastery Gate #4:** answer a question live.

## 1:25 — 1:30 · Close
- Everyone says one thing they noticed improve from round 1 to round 4. About themselves or a peer.
