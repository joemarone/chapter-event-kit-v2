# Materials — Actions Speak Louder

**Group size:** 8–12 kids
**Estimated cost:** $10–15 total, nearly all reusable
**Sourcing time:** 5 minutes at any dollar store or office supply

---

## The essentials

- [ ] **Index cards — 1 pack.** 3×5 unlined. Each kid uses 3–5 cards for story outlines.
- [ ] **Sharpies, assorted.** Fine point. Kids write 3 words per section of their story — big and bold.
- [ ] **A soft ball or beanbag.** For the warm-up circle. Has to be catchable one-handed by the smallest kid.
- [ ] **Timer or phone with a stopwatch.** Visible to the whole room. The 60-second rule is the whole workshop — it needs to be obvious.
- [ ] **Masking tape — 1 roll.** Mark a "speaker's spot" on the floor so kids know where to stand.

## Nice-to-haves

- [ ] A second timer (mobile, so kids can time themselves during pair practice)
- [ ] A small bell or chime to signal "30 seconds left"
- [ ] Sticky notes for audience questions during Q&A round

## Tools (stay with the organizer)

- [ ] Clipboard with the Mastery sign-off chart
- [ ] Printed list of kids' names so you can track who's gone and who hasn't
- [ ] Phone to record kids (optional, chapter decides — get parent permission first)

## What NOT to bring

- Microphones or audio gear. Kids need to project with their own voices — that's the muscle we're building.
- Scripts or printed stories. Story is verbal, not written, for this one.
- Snacks. They become the entire workshop. Save for after.

---

## Setup tip

Arrange chairs in a wide U-shape, not rows. Every kid can see every other kid's face. The "stage" is the opening of the U, with the masking tape spot on the floor. No podium, no table — the kid's body is the whole instrument.
