## Mastery — what every kid walks out able to do

Workshops are a blast — and every kid walks out having actually done something. Below is the bar.

1. Deliver a 60-second story with a clear beginning, middle, and end — no filler words.
2. Hold eye contact with at least three audience members.
3. Recover from one deliberate interruption without losing the thread.
4. Take a question from the audience and answer it on the spot.
