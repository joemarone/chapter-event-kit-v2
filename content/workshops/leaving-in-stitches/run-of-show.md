# Run-of-Show — Leaving in Stitches

**Total runtime: 90 minutes**

---

## Before kids arrive (10 min prep)

- [ ] Pre-cut felt into 4×4 squares — 3 per kid.
- [ ] Pre-thread 3 needles per kid with ~18" of embroidery thread (this saves 15 minutes of chaos).
- [ ] Lay out one "finished example" object — a felt pouch, a pincushion, or a felt cookie — so kids see what they're aiming at.
- [ ] Set chairs around tables, not in rows. Sewing is social.

## 0:00 — 0:10 · Demo and grip

- Kids sit around the tables.
- Show the example. Pass it around. Kids can squeeze it, flex it.
- **Teach the needle hold:** thumb and index only. The needle is a tool, not a sword.
- **Mastery Gate #1:** every kid threads their own needle and ties a starting knot. You help once, max — then they do the second one themselves.

## 0:10 — 0:20 · The running stitch drill

- On a scrap square, each kid does a practice row of 10 running stitches.
- Count out loud together: "In, out, in, out." Rhythm matters more than speed.
- **Mastery Gate #2:** 10 stitches, roughly evenly spaced. Not perfect — even.

## 0:20 — 0:70 · Build the real thing (50 min)

- Kids pick one of three projects:
  - **Felt pouch** (two squares, sewn on three sides, open on the fourth)
  - **Stuffed shape** (heart, star, or their own outline — sewn 2/3 around, then stuffed and closed)
  - **Button bracelet** (felt strip with 3 buttons sewn on)
- Sit side-by-side, not across. Kids copy each other's technique — this is the point.
- **Your job as organizer:**
  1. When a kid gets stuck, don't fix it. Point to another kid who's past that part.
  2. Rescue tangles — only tangles. Tangles kill motivation.
  3. At the 30-min mark: "Whoever's finished step 1, show your neighbor how you did it." This triggers Mastery Gate #4.

## 0:70 — 0:80 · Show table

- Everyone places their finished object on one central table.
- Walk the table as a group. Each kid says one thing: "I made [X]. The tricky part was [Y]."
- No critiques. Just noticing.

## 0:80 — 0:90 · Close

- **Mastery Gate #3:** before leaving, every kid's object must be **functional** — the pouch holds something, the shape is stuffed and closed, the bracelet fits on a wrist. If it's not functional yet, 5 more minutes at the table with you.
- Kids pack up scraps in a small bag — they take those home too. Leftover felt is gold for rainy days.

## Common failures and fast fixes

- **"My thread keeps coming out of the needle"** → The knot's on the wrong end, or it's too small. Re-tie a double knot.
- **"I poked myself"** → Happens. Band-aid in the tool kit. Show them the safer grip.
- **"Mine looks messy"** → Good. Handmade things aren't machine-made. Show yours — it's messy too.

## Clean-up (5 min, off the clock)

- Needles back in the pincushion, counted. No needles left loose on the table. Ever.
