# Materials — Leaving in Stitches

**Group size:** 8–12 kids
**Estimated cost:** $30–40 total, mostly reusable across multiple workshops
**Sourcing time:** 20 minutes at a craft store (Michaels, JoAnn) or one Amazon order

---

## The essentials

- [ ] **Felt squares, assorted colors — 30+.** 9×12 sheets are fine; cut down to 4×4 pre-workshop. Stiff craft felt, not wool. Kids need something stable.
- [ ] **Embroidery thread — 1 pack (assorted colors).** DMC or similar. Pre-cut into ~18" lengths. Longer tangles, shorter runs out.
- [ ] **Blunt-tip needles (plastic or metal) — 3 per kid.** Size 18 tapestry needles work great — the eye is big enough for small fingers.
- [ ] **Kid-safe scissors — 1 per kid.** Blunt tips. Nothing fancy.
- [ ] **Stuffing/cotton — 1 small bag.** Polyfil pillow stuffing.
- [ ] **Buttons — a mixed jar.** Assorted sizes. Thrift stores are gold for this.

## Nice-to-haves

- [ ] Fabric markers or chalk (for kids who want to draw their stitch line before sewing)
- [ ] Small safety pins (to hold two pieces together while sewing)
- [ ] A pincushion or two — looks nice, keeps needles accounted for

## Tools (stay with the organizer)

- [ ] Sharper adult scissors for emergency cuts
- [ ] Band-aids (a couple — small pokes happen, it's fine)
- [ ] Clipboard with Mastery sign-off chart
- [ ] A finished example object — pouch, stuffed heart, or button bracelet — to show kids what they're aiming at

## What NOT to bring

- Sewing machines. This workshop is hand-sewing; machines defeat the point (and are dangerous for 5–9 year olds).
- Sharp needles. Blunt tips only. The fabric is soft enough.
- Complicated patterns. The project is squares + buttons + stitches. Patterns kill flow.

---

## Setup tip

Pre-cut and pre-thread **everything** before kids arrive. A workshop that starts with 15 minutes of tangled thread becomes a workshop about untangling thread. Put each kid's needles into a small felt scrap so they're accounted for at a glance.
