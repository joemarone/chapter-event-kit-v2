## Mastery — what every kid walks out able to do

Workshops are a blast — and every kid walks out having actually done something. Below is the bar.

1. Thread a needle and tie a starting knot without help.
2. Sew a straight seam of at least 10 stitches with consistent spacing.
3. Finish a real, functional object you can take home and use.
4. Teach the running stitch to one classmate who's stuck.
